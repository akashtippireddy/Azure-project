# Task 3: Design the Commitment Strategy

## Goal

Choose the most suitable purchasing model for each workload.

## Decision Guidance

- Use a Reserved Instance when the resource family, region, and term are sufficiently predictable.
- Use a Savings Plan for Compute when spend is stable but VM family or region may change.
- Keep variable, short-lived, or uncertain usage on pay-as-you-go pricing.

## Activities

1. Compare one-year and three-year commitment options.
2. Calculate break-even utilization and expected savings.
3. Model partial coverage so a flexible buffer remains.
4. Document cancellation, exchange, scope, and payment considerations.
5. Obtain finance and workload-owner approval.

## Deliverables

- Commitment decision matrix.
- Purchase quantities or hourly commitment targets.
- Approval record and implementation schedule.
