# Task 4: Implement the Purchase

## Goal

Apply the approved reservations and Savings Plan commitments with appropriate access controls.

## Activities

1. Confirm the target scope, billing account, subscription, and term.
2. Validate the final quantity or hourly commitment immediately before purchase.
3. Purchase through the Azure portal or an approved automation workflow.
4. Capture transaction details, effective date, and cost center.
5. Confirm that eligible usage is receiving the expected benefit.

## Controls

- Use a documented approval before financial commitment.
- Restrict purchase permissions through RBAC.
- Store purchase records in the project evidence folder.
- Do not include secrets or payment details in source control.

## Deliverables

- Purchase confirmation.
- Updated commitment inventory.
- Initial post-purchase validation report.
