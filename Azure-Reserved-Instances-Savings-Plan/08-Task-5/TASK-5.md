
# Task 5: Monitor and Optimize

## Goal

Ensure commitments remain utilized and continue to produce measurable savings.

## Activities

1. Review reservation utilization and Savings Plan coverage monthly.
2. Compare actual savings with the original forecast.
3. Investigate unused commitment, scope mismatch, or workload changes.
4. Track migrations, scaling changes, and expiring terms.
5. Adjust future purchases based on observed demand and business plans.

## Key Metrics

- Utilization percentage
- Coverage percentage
- Effective hourly rate
- Gross and net savings
- Unused commitment cost
- Forecast variance

## Deliverables

- Monthly optimization report.
- Renewal and expiration calendar.
- Actions for underutilized or insufficient commitments.
